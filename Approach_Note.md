We use EfficientNet-B0 to extract features, projecting them into a 128D L2-normalized embedding space. To enforce color-invariance, we apply aggressive augmentations (RandomGrayscale, Hue/Saturation ColorJitter), forcing the model to learn structural and textural design features. Training optimizes Cross-Entropy over normalized embeddings, establishing a strong metric space where cosine similarity effectively ranks gallery matches (Identification) and classifies same-design pairs (Verification).
